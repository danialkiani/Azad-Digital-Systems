Ball Boss — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Ball Boss is made by Azad Digital Systems.

Ball Boss does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no chat, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your game progress (coins, levels, settings) is saved only on your own device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

On iPhone, if you choose to use Apple Game Center, your scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

If this policy changes (for example, if we add advertising), we will update this page and the store listing before the change goes live.
