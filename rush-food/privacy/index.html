Rush Food: Kitchen Empire — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Rush Food: Kitchen Empire is made by Azad Digital Systems Ltd.

We don't collect any personal data

The game works completely offline. It has no accounts, no chat and no connection to the internet, and it does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, levels, restaurants and settings) and the birth year you enter on first launch are saved only on your own device, so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device. If your phone's backup is switched on, your phone may include your game progress in your own personal backup, which is controlled by Google or Apple and your device settings.

Ads and purchases

This version of the game has no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future update adds them, we will update this page and the store listing before the change goes live.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes all of its saved data.
