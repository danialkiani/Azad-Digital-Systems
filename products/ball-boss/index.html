About the game

Number skills, turned into a 3D adventure

Ball Boss turns number skills into a fast, exciting 3D adventure. Swipe to throw your ball, then steer it down the track. Roll into a ball with the same number and they merge: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, all the way to millions, billions and beyond.

Every few seconds there's a decision to make. Will you take the ×3 gate or the +half gate? Is your number big enough to smash that wall? Can you open a door that only lets multiples of 3 through? Choose well and your ball grows. Choose badly and it shrinks.

Travel through 9 worlds, from Sunny Park and Candy Land to Neon City, Volcano Run and the Cosmic Highway. Fill up FEVER mode to turn gold and smash everything, catch slow-motion highlight replays, and beat the giant Boss Ball at the end of each level.

Then take on Infinity mode. You get 3 hearts, the track keeps getting faster, and you win Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond medals. How far can you roll?

Why parents like it

Smart choices beat fast thumbs

Ball Boss rewards thinking, not just reactions, so every run is real number practice.

  • Practises doubling, multiplication, division and comparing large numbers.
  • Teaches big-number names in a fun way: thousand, million, billion, trillion… centillion.
  • Includes number rules such as multiples of 3, powers of 2 and even numbers.
  • Explains mistakes kindly: a robot helper called Dot explains why ÷2 made the ball smaller.
  • Rewards smart choices, not just fast reactions.
The story behind it

Ball Boss was designed with real kid input. The ball colours, from white 2s to the shimmering centillion balls, were chosen by a young player in our own family, and the game was tried and tuned by the family as it was built.
